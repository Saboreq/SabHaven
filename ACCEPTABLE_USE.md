# SabHaven Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-20

Use SabHaven only for content you are legally entitled to possess and share.

Prohibited use includes:

- illegal content or activity;
- malware, ransomware, credential-stealing material or malicious payloads;
- infringement of copyright, privacy, confidentiality or other rights without lawful authorization;
- harassment, threats, fraud, impersonation or exploitation;
- bypassing authentication, authorization, storage policies or account boundaries;
- denial-of-service, destructive testing or activity intended to degrade availability;
- using another person's credentials or accessing content without authorization;
- misleading claims about the ownership, origin or legality of content.

The operator may take proportionate action including restriction, removal, suspension, evidence preservation or legally required disclosure.

Report abuse or illegal content to **contact@saboreq.xyz** or use https://files.saboreq.xyz/abuse.
