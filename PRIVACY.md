# SabHaven Privacy Policy

**Effective version:** 2026-09-20

This policy applies to the hosted SabHaven service at https://files.saboreq.xyz. Self-hosted deployments are controlled by their own operators.

## Controller

**Operator:** Saboreq  
**Contact:** contact@saboreq.xyz

## Data processed

SabHaven may process account email and user ID, account role, invite and redemption records, file and folder metadata, uploaded files, legal-acceptance records, support/abuse correspondence, and technical/security data processed by infrastructure providers such as request metadata and IP addresses.

## Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to provide and administer the service, authenticate accounts, store and deliver files, enforce permissions, provide support, prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, maintain the service and comply with law. Where GDPR applies, processing may rely on performance of the service contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, legal obligations, and consent when a separate optional feature specifically requires it.

## Providers

The hosted service uses Supabase for authentication/database/storage, Vercel for application hosting and delivery, and Cloudflare where used for DNS, network or security services. Processing locations and transfer safeguards depend on provider configuration and applicable agreements.

## Retention

Account and content data is generally retained while active. Eligible users can delete their account through the hosted service, which attempts to remove their stored files and account-linked database records. Provider backups/security logs and information required for legal, fraud-prevention, security or dispute purposes may remain for a limited period.

## Browser storage

SabHaven uses browser storage and similar strictly functional technologies for authentication/session continuity. The application does not currently include advertising or marketing trackers.

## Rights

Where GDPR applies, users may have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Requests can be sent to contact@saboreq.xyz. Users may also complain to the competent supervisory authority, including Prezes UODO in Poland where applicable.

## Security

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep credentials confidential and do not upload information you are not authorized to store or share.

For the most current hosted-service notice, see https://files.saboreq.xyz/privacy.
